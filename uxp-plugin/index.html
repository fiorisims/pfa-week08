How it works

Build Template places your images as panels on one page. Tick panels, then pick a mood: each panel gets its own Gradient Map adjustment layer, clipped to that panel, so you can edit the stops in Properties or change the blend later. Strength is the layer's opacity and moves while you drag. Colour panels get a Color-blend map that keeps their light and shadow; grayscale panels get a full gradient map; line art maps the ink to the shadow colour and the paper to the light colour. Split into bands writes three maps per panel (shadows, midtones, highlights) with their own strength. Describe a mood in words and the panel blends the moods it recognises; the status line says what it understood. Lock palette reuses one palette across panels; Reroll makes another variation.
